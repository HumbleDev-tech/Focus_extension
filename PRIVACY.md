# Privacy Policy for Libertad

**Effective Date:** September 11, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 14, 2026

**Libertad** ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

* **No Personal Data Collected**: Libertad does **not** collect, store, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, IP address, browsing history, or keystrokes).
* **Local Settings Storage**: Your focus preferences (e.g. which elements to hide, selected focus preset) are saved strictly in your browser's local synchronization storage (`chrome.storage.sync`). This data remains solely within your browser account and is never accessible to us or any third parties.

---

## 2. Permissions & Third-Party Services

* **Storage Permission (`storage`)**: Used exclusively to remember your selected focus level and toggle choices across browser sessions.
* **Scripting Permission (`scripting`)**: Used solely to programmatically inject and activate content scripts on already-open YouTube tabs immediately upon installation or update without requiring a manual page reload.
* **Return YouTube Dislike API**: If the "Restore Dislikes" feature is enabled, the extension sends the public YouTube Video ID to `https://returnyoutubedislikeapi.com` solely to retrieve the aggregated like/dislike counts. No cookies, user identifiers, or personal data are included with these requests.
* **YouTube oEmbed Service**: If the "Untranslate Titles" feature is enabled, public YouTube video URLs are queried via YouTube's public oEmbed endpoint solely to retrieve the creator's original video title in their native language. No user credentials, cookies, browsing history, or personal identifiers are transmitted with these requests.
* **SponsorBlock API (`https://sponsor.ajay.app`)**: If the "Skip In-Video Sponsors" feature is enabled, the public YouTube Video ID is sent to the SponsorBlock public API solely to retrieve community-submitted segment timestamps (sponsorships, self-promotions, and interaction reminders). No cookies, user identifiers, accounts, or personal data are included with these requests.
* **No Analytics or Trackers**: Libertad contains zero telemetry, analytics, tracking scripts, or advertisements.
